## Key Takeaways

- AWS offers a variety of specialized services that address specific use cases beyond core compute, storage, and networking. These services can significantly reduce operational overhead when used appropriately.

- Services such as AppStream 2.0 and WorkSpaces support end-user computing by enabling remote application and desktop delivery.

- Migration-related services including DataSync, Migration Hub, Migration Evaluator, and Elastic Disaster Recovery (DRS) help organizations plan, execute, and manage migrations to AWS more effectively.

- Developer-focused services like Amplify and Device Farm accelerate frontend and mobile application development and testing.

- Specialized services such as IoT Core and Ground Station address niche industry needs, including Internet of Things device management and satellite communications.

- Understanding the purpose of these services helps in making well-informed architectural decisions, even if they are not used in every environment.
