## Key Takeaways
- Improved pacing on this exam (47 minutes vs 25 minutes on the previous retake), showing better discipline.
- Cloud Concepts section remains a weak area, especially with “Select two” and “Select three” style questions.
- AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) has six perspectives: Business, People, Governance, Platform, Security, and Operations. Questions about organizational transformation and customer responsiveness fall under the Business perspective.
- When migrating to AWS, both AWS Professional Services and APN Consulting Partners can assist, but they are different (one is AWS’s internal team, the other is external approved partners).
- The six advantages of cloud computing are frequently tested. The most common correct answers are trading capital expense for variable expense, stopping guessing about capacity, and benefiting from massive economies of scale.
- In a VPC, Security Groups only support allow rules and operate at the instance level. Network ACLs support both allow and deny rules and operate at the subnet level.
- AWS Support Plans: Basic and Developer plans only provide access to core Trusted Advisor checks. Business and higher plans provide access to all Trusted Advisor checks.
