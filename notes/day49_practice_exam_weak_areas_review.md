## Key Takeaways
- The Envision phase of the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework focuses on demonstrating how the cloud can accelerate business outcomes.
- Security Groups operate at the instance level and only support allow rules, while Network ACLs operate at the subnet level and support both allow and deny rules.
- A VPC is regional, while subnets are tied to a specific Availability Zone.
- AWS IAM and Amazon CloudFront are global services, while most other services (such as EC2, S3, and VPC) are regional.
- Business Support is the most cost-effective plan that provides 24x7 phone support. A dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM) is only available with the Enterprise Support plan.
- The three primary ways to access and manage AWS services are the AWS Management Console, AWS CLI, and AWS SDK.
