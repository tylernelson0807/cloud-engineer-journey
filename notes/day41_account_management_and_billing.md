## Key Takeaways

- AWS Organizations and Control Tower are foundational for managing multiple AWS accounts at scale, enabling centralized governance, security policies, and consolidated billing.

- Services such as RAM and Service Catalog help organizations share resources securely and provide teams with approved, standardized infrastructure templates while maintaining control.

- Understanding AWS pricing models (On-Demand, Reserved, Savings Plans, and Spot) is essential for making cost-effective decisions based on workload characteristics and usage patterns.

- Tools like Compute Optimizer, Cost Anomaly Detection, and the AWS Pricing Calculator support proactive cost optimization and help prevent unexpected spending.

- Trusted Advisor provides actionable recommendations across cost, performance, security, and fault tolerance, while Service Quotas and Support Plans help manage resource limits and access technical assistance when needed.

- Effective account management and billing practices are critical for maintaining financial control, operational efficiency, and security as AWS environments grow in size and complexity.
