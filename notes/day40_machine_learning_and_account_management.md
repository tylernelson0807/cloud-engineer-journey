## Key Takeaways

- AWS provides both pre-trained AI services (such as Rekognition and Comprehend) that can be implemented quickly with minimal machine learning expertise, as well as SageMaker for building, training, and deploying custom machine learning models at scale.

- Understanding when to use pre-trained AI services versus building custom models is important for making practical and cost-effective decisions.

- AWS Organizations and Control Tower are essential for managing multiple AWS accounts at scale, enabling centralized governance, security policies, and consolidated billing.

- Cost management tools such as Compute Optimizer, Cost Anomaly Detection, and the AWS Pricing Calculator help organizations optimize spending and avoid unexpected costs.

- Trusted Advisor, Service Quotas, and Support Plans play important roles in operational excellence, resource management, and obtaining technical assistance when needed.

- Effective account management and billing practices are critical for maintaining control, security, and cost efficiency as AWS environments grow in size and complexity.
